## Observed design patterns used

- Full-screen immersive hero scene.
- Editorial display typography with restrained operational typography.
- Scroll-led narrative with alternating light and dark environments.
- Interactive explanatory visualization.
- Orbital forms, soft depth, controlled highlights, and a restrained gold
  accent.
- Motion concentrated in one memorable scene and a few state transitions.

## Translation into Comet Vault

- particles → dormant comets;
- 3D orbital scene → dependency-free 2D canvas field;
- interactive science diagrams → selectable idea-cluster inspection;
- research narrative → orientation/holding narrative;
- research provenance → permanent source-state rail.

The resulting interface remains static, bilingual, dependency-free, and
non-activating.
